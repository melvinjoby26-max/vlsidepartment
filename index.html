Center of Excellence

Pioneering Microelectronics & Chip Design

The EE - VLSI Design and Technology program is committed to developing industry-ready engineers capable of designing complex modern integrated circuits (ICs), high-speed digital processors, analog/RF mixed-signal interfaces, and custom AI accelerator hardware.

Through hands-on workshops, faculty-guided tapeout programs, and regular industry masterclasses, students gain end-to-end expertise spanning architectural exploration, RTL verification, physical design, and post-silicon validation.

RTL & ASIC Design

Verilog, SystemVerilog, UVM, and synthesis flows

Analog & Mixed Signal

Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, and RF layout design

FPGA Acceleration

AMD Xilinx Vivado, PYNQ, and Vitis HLS

Tapeout Opportunities

Shuttle tapeouts via commercial & open-source PDKs

Advanced VLSI CAD Laboratories

Equipped with tier-1 semiconductor software licenses and high-compute clusters for all department workshops:

Cadence Innovus Cadence Virtuoso Cadence Genus Synopsys VCS Synopsys Design Compiler AMD Xilinx Vivado ML Siemens QuestaSim OpenROAD / SkyWater 130
